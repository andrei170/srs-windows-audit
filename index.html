You Have Two Websites. One Of Them Sends Every Caller To 07890 000000.

Prepared for Toni & Stephen Stewart · S.R.S Windows Ltd · 17 September 2026 · AG1 Consulting
2
websites · two phone numbers · one of them a placeholder

You want more work and you have taken on staff to handle it. Before any advertising, the first job is making sure the people already finding you can actually reach you.

Right now there are two S.R.S Windows websites. The older one tells visitors to ring 07890 000000 — a dummy number that connects to nobody. The newer one works, but only sells one product and cannot measure a single enquiry.

Both sites read 17 September 2026 · company record checked at Companies House
Finding 1 — The Older Site Dials A Number That Does Not Exist
srs-windows.co.uk (with the hyphen) Phone shown and dialled: 07890 000000 (tel:+447890000000) — 5 links Footer: "built and managed by Trades Geeks" srswindows.co.uk (no hyphen — the newer site) Phone shown and dialled: 07889 631 319
Anyone who finds the older site cannot ring you

07890 000000 is a placeholder — the kind of number a website template ships with before the real one is added. Every homeowner who lands on that site and taps "call" reaches nobody, and you would never know they tried. The site is managed by another company, so it is worth asking them to fix the number today, or taking the site down.

Finding 2 — Two Sites, Saying Two Different Things
Older site (hyphen) Newer site (no hyphen) Sells Windows and conservatories Tiled conservatory roofs only Phone 07890 000000 07889 631 319 Accreditation shown FENSA CERTASS 12-1432 Reviews "380+ verified customer reviews" None shown Built by Trades Geeks Not stated
Google and customers do not know which one is really you

Two sites with different numbers, different products and different claims split whatever authority you have between them, and make a careful customer wonder which is genuine. The claims on the older site — the review count, the years, the guarantee — need to be checked before anyone relies on them, because we could not match them to public records, and published claims have to be accurate.

Finding 3 — The Newer Site Only Sells One Thing
Pages on srswindows.co.uk: 17 Main service: Lightweight tiled conservatory roofs Town pages: 5 — Medway, Maidstone, Dartford, Gravesend, Sittingbourne Guides: 7 Windows: not mentioned Doors: not mentioned New conservatories / repairs: not mentioned
It is well built — for one product

The newer site is genuinely tidy: fast, town pages, a postcode checker, a clean form, CERTASS shown clearly. But a business called S.R.S Windows has a website that never mentions windows. If you want more work for the team you have just taken on, the site has to sell the full range you actually install.

Finding 4 — Nothing Is Measured, And No Reviews Are Shown
Newer site tracking: none ("This site sets no cookies") Google Ads tag: none Meta pixel: none Reviews on the site: none Warm Roof Division (Medway): Google 5.0 from 75 reviews, Which? Trusted Traders 4.9 from 36
Word of mouth works — but it is invisible online

Your work comes from word of mouth, which means happy customers exist. None of them appear on either website. Meanwhile the conservatory roof firm down the road shows 75 Google reviews. And with no tracking, if you start advertising tomorrow you will not be able to tell which adverts brought in which jobs.

What We Would Do, In Order
  • Fix or remove the placeholder number today. Any call from that site currently goes nowhere.
  • One website. Pick the newer, better-built site and point the older address at it.
  • Sell the full range — windows, doors, conservatories and roofs — with the town pages extended to each.
  • Put real reviews on the site and start asking every finished job for one.
  • Install tracking, so every call and form is counted before a penny goes on advertising.
  • Then advertise across Medway and Kent, measured on booked surveys, not clicks.

Twenty years of trading. The first fix costs nothing.

Fix what is losing calls first, then bring in the work for the new team.
